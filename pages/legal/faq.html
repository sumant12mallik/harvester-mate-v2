FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions ke jawab

Kaise register karu?
▼
Login page pe "Register Now" tap karo. User ya Harvester Owner choose karo, apna name, mobile, village aur password daalo. Owner registration ke liye admin approval zaroori hai.
Owner approval kitne time me milta hai?
▼
Usually 24-48 hours me admin aapka registration review karta hai. Approve hone ke baad aap login kar sakte ho.
Timer kaise kaam karta hai?
▼
Time Calculation screen pe 2 Wheel aur 4 Wheel cards hai. Jis wheel ka timer chalana hai, us card pe tap karo, phir play button dabao. Dono timers alag alag chal sakte hai.
Rate kaise change karu?
▼
Time Calculation screen pe "Rates (Per Hour)" section hai. Wahan 2 Wheel aur 4 Wheel ka rate type karke change kar sakte ho. Har customer ke liye alag rate bhi set kar sakte ho.
Online payment kaise enable karu?
▼
Online payment Razorpay ke through hota hai. Ye feature tab tak disabled hai jab tak admin Razorpay keys setup nahi karta. Setup hone ke baad "Payment Mode" me "Online" option dikhega.
Purane season ka customer kaise search karu?
▼
Search page pe jaake mobile number ya naam type karo. Result me current season ke saath purane season ke dues bhi dikhenge.
Offline data safe hai?
▼
Haan. Jab aap offline hote ho, saara data phone me safely save hota hai. Internet aane par automatically Firebase me sync ho jata hai. Aapka data kabhi loss nahi hota.
Account delete kaise karu?
▼
Settings page pe "Delete My Account" button hai. Ya email bhejo delete@harvestermate.app pe. 30 din me saara data permanently delete ho jayega.
Customer ko WhatsApp message kaise bheju?
▼
Jab customer save karo, WhatsApp automatically open hoga prefilled message ke saath. Customer detail page pe "Message" button bhi hai.
Aur koi problem hai to kya karu?
▼
Support team se contact karo: support@harvestermate.app ya call +91 99999 99999.
