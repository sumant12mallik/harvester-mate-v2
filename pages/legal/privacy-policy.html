Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Introduction

HarvesterMate ("we", "our", "app") harvester owners aur unke customers ke liye business management app hai. Ye privacy policy batati hai ki hum kaunsi information collect karte hai, kaise use karte hai, aur kaise protect karte hai.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Info: Name, mobile number, village, password
  • Customer Data: Customer name, mobile, village, work details
  • Business Data: Diesel, expenses, dealer transactions
  • Device Info: Device type, OS version

3. How We Use Data

  • App functionality provide karne ke liye
  • Customer ko WhatsApp par updates bhejne ke liye
  • Payment processing ke liye (Razorpay)
  • Business reports generate karne ke liye

4. Data Storage & Security

Aapka data Google Firebase me securely store hota hai. Data encrypted hai. Hum aapka data kisi third party ko sell nahi karte.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — Auth, Database, Storage
  • Razorpay — Payment processing
  • WhatsApp — Message sending

6. Your Rights

Aap kabhi bhi apna account delete kar sakte ho — Settings → Delete Account. Saara data 30 din me permanently delete ho jayega.

7. Children's Privacy

Ye app 18+ business users ke liye hai. Hum 13 saal se kam bachon ka data intentionally collect nahi karte.
