HarvesterMate

For a Better Harvest Tomorrow

Version 2.0.0

Hamara Mission

HarvesterMate ka mission hai harvester owners aur unke customers ke beech business ko aasaan, transparent aur digital banana. Hum chahte hai ki koi bhi harvester owner apna pura business ek hi app me manage kar sake.

Key Features

  • ⏱️ 2W/4W Independent Time Calculator
  • 👥 Complete Customer Management
  • 💰 Online Payments (Razorpay)
  • 📱 WhatsApp Auto Messages
  • ⛽ Diesel & Repair Tracking
  • 💵 Personal Expense Management
  • 🤝 Dealer Ledger
  • 👤 Driver Attendance
  • 📊 Season Reports & Analytics
  • 🔄 Offline Support

Kiske Liye

Ye app specially Indian harvester owners ke liye design kiya gaya hai, jo chahte hai apna business professionally manage karna. Har season ka alag record, har customer ka hisaab, aur har rupee ka track.
