Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Overview

HarvesterMate khud koi payment process nahi karta — payments directly harvester owner aur customer ke beech hote hai. Refund policy mutual agreement pe depend karti hai.

2. Online Payments (Razorpay)

Agar payment fail ho jaye par customer ke account se paisa cut ho jaye:

  • Auto-refund 5-7 business days me process hoga
  • Refund original payment method pe jayega
  • Bank charges applicable ho sakte hai

3. Disputed Work Charges

Agar customer ko kaam me issue hai:

  1. Pehle harvester owner se direct baat karo
  2. Agar solve na ho, to support se contact karo
  3. Hum mediation karenge

4. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Kaam complete hone ke baad
  • Customer ne galat amount diya par work accept kiya
  • Cash payments (offline)

5. Dispute Resolution

Koi bhi dispute 7 working days me resolve karne ki koshish karenge.
